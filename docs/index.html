About PZPL

PZPL (OPC) Private Limited is pioneering hyperlocal service platforms across India.

With 20 years of software development expertise spanning London (2011-2023) and Mumbai (2004-2011), founder Ashish Kumar Sinha brings global technology standards to local Indian markets.

"By the people, for the people" — Our applications reflect how Indian communities naturally operate: direct, trust-based, and locally rooted, eliminating middlemen and ensuring genuine community connections.
