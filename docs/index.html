About PZPL

PZPL (OPC) Private Limited is building India's first unified hyperlocal service super-app — connecting people to skills, items, live prices, and life events within their own neighbourhood.

With 20 years of software development expertise spanning London (2011–2023) and Mumbai (2004–2011), founder Ashish Kumar Sinha brings global technology standards to local Indian markets. PZPL is recognized on the Startup India platform.

"By the people, for the people" — Our applications reflect how Indian communities naturally operate: direct, trust-based, and locally rooted, eliminating middlemen and ensuring genuine community connections.

How It Works

Simple, Scalable, Hyperlocal

One phone number = one registration. No clutter. No middlemen. Direct community connections within 1–30 KM.

🏗️

Value-Added Services

Jobs · Coming Soon: Construction Planner

Next-generation services built on top of the Find Local platform. Jobs connects job posters with nearby workers through skill-based posting, location-aware search, and one-tap apply — with real-time notifications to both parties.

Example: A contractor posts a "Plumber needed" job → nearby registered plumbers receive instant notifications and can apply with one tap.
🔧

Skill-Based Platforms

Mechanics · Events · Legals

One phone number registers one skill. Users search and instantly find nearby skilled workers' contact numbers within 1–30 KM radius.

Example: A plumber registers → appears in search results for anyone within 30 KM looking for a plumber.
📦

Item-Based Platforms

Rentals · Shadi · Scrap · Office

One phone number registers one item or listing. Users search and find nearby listings — rental properties, bride/groom profiles, scrap items, or office contacts — within 1–30 KM.

Example: A landlord lists a 2BHK flat → appears in rental searches for users nearby.
💰

Live Pricing Platform

Google Sheet–Powered · Real-Time

Shops link their Google Sheet with item names and prices. Any change in the sheet reflects live in search results instantly. Users search and compare real-time prices from nearby shops within 1–50 KM.

Covers: Construction materials, hardware, electrical, agricultural, jewelry, handmade items, and more.
