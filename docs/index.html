About PZPL

PZPL (OPC) Private Limited is building India's first hyperlocal circular economy platform — Find Local (fdlocal.in) — connecting skilled local workers, small shops, and communities within a 50 km radius, eliminating middlemen and the need to migrate to cities.

⚠️
The Problem

Local economies are fragmented — skilled workers migrate to cities despite nearby demand, small shops have no digital presence, and 600M+ Indians in Tier-2/3 towns have no unified platform for services, live prices, rentals, or matrimonial matches within their own neighbourhood.

✅
Our Solution

Find Local unifies 9 hyperlocal platforms in one super-app. Workers register with one phone number; shops go live with a Google Sheet. Zero middlemen, zero commissions, instantly discoverable within 1–50 km in 12 Indian languages on Android & Web.

"By the people, for the people" — Our applications reflect how Indian communities naturally operate: direct, trust-based, and locally rooted — eliminating middlemen and ensuring genuine community connections.

Skill-Based Platforms

Mechanics · Events · Legals

One phone number registers one skill. Users search and instantly find nearby skilled workers' contact numbers within 1–30 KM radius.

Example: A plumber registers → appears in search results for anyone within 30 KM looking for a plumber.
📦

Item-Based Platforms

Rentals · Shadi · Scrap · Office

One phone number registers one item or listing. Users search and find nearby listings — rental properties, bride/groom profiles, scrap items, or office contacts — within 1–30 KM.

Example: A landlord lists a 2BHK flat → appears in rental searches for users nearby.
