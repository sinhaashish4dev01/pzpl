About PZPL

PZPL Private Limited is building India's first hyperlocal circular economy platform — Find Local — connecting skilled local workers, small shops, and communities within a 50 km radius, eliminating middlemen and the need to migrate to cities.

⚠️
The Problem

Local economies are fragmented — skilled workers migrate to cities despite nearby demand, small shops have no digital presence, and 600M+ Indians in Tier-2/3 towns have no unified platform for services, live prices, rentals, or matrimonial matches within their own neighbourhood.

✅
Our Solution

Find Local is being developed to unify 9 hyperlocal platforms in one super-app. Workers will register with one phone number; shops will update pricing via Google Sheet. Zero middlemen, zero commissions, with planned discovery within 1–50 km in 12 Indian languages on Android & Web.

"By the people, for the people" — Our applications reflect how Indian communities naturally operate: direct, trust-based, and locally rooted — eliminating middlemen and ensuring genuine community connections.
